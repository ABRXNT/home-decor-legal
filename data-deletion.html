Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 24, 2026

About Our Application

Home Decor is a privately operated application used to manage and schedule content for its associated Facebook Page.

Personal Data

Home Decor does not collect or store personal information from Facebook Page followers, visitors, or members of the general public.

The application processes authentication credentials and technical information required for authorized publishing operations.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you have questions about information associated with Home Decor or wish to request deletion of information that we may hold, please contact:

anate.import@gmail.com

Include "Home Decor Data Deletion Request" in the email subject and describe the information you want deleted. Please do not send passwords or access tokens.

We will review the request and respond within 30 days. Where applicable, we will delete information under our control or explain why deletion cannot be completed.

Facebook Data

Information managed directly by Facebook is subject to Meta's own privacy policies and account controls.
