Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. About Home Decor

Home Decor is a privately operated content management application used to prepare, manage, and schedule posts for its associated Facebook Page.

2. Information We Collect

Home Decor does not collect personal information from Facebook Page followers, visitors, or members of the general public.

The application processes authentication credentials and technical information necessary to perform authorized publishing operations through the Meta Graph API.

3. How Information Is Used

Authentication and technical information is used solely to manage and schedule content on the authorized Facebook Page.

4. Information Sharing

Home Decor does not sell personal information. Information necessary for authorized publishing operations is transmitted to Meta through its official APIs.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect authentication credentials and restrict access to the application.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Technical and authentication information is retained only as needed for authorized application operations. Access credentials may be revoked when no longer needed.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: anate.import@gmail.com

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality or applicable requirements.
